# Design notes

## M2 — fully accepted resampling trials

- Match-order trials yield two distinct diagnostics: a provisional rotation may exist even when the one-shot initialization rejects, whereas a fully accepted trial passes every geometry and map-quality gate. `pose_stability_valid_candidates` includes both kinds of rotation for the angular statistic; `pose_stability_provisional_candidates` counts rejected trials with a rotation; `pose_stability_accepted_trials` counts full successes. The current acceptance rule requires at least 4 candidate rotations, median rotation disagreement ≤10°, and at least 4 fully accepted trials among the eight fixed permutations. Provisional rotations never satisfy the accepted-trial minimum. An isolated failure of that minimum returns `insufficient_accepted_pose_stability_trials` and clears public pose/map fields while keeping diagnostic evidence. Setting trials to zero bypasses the check.
- The corrected gate keeps all 12 fixed KITTI decisions, with accepted trials 8/8, 7/8, 7/8, and 7/8 on the four accepted original pairs and 8/8 on the accepted held-out pair. The 20→25 anomaly remains rejected for 42.36° disagreement, with only 1/8 trials fully accepted. Synthetic weak-baseline acceptance becomes 0/5 rather than 2/5. Two existing physically legitimate synthetic scenes reject: small translation with moderate rotation has 2/8 accepted trials and 1.92° median angular disagreement; planar geometry has 1/8 and 8.23°. Most rejected trials fail cheirality support or minimum pose-inlier count. Thus a stable provisional orientation does not imply a stable fully reconstructible map. No thresholds were relaxed or ground truth added to the estimator.
- An offline truth check with OpenCV 4.8.1 found that the small-translation one-shot pose errs by 1.212° in rotation and 10.065° in translation direction; a rejected permutation has 172.056° direction error. The planar one-shot pose errs by 3.798°/24.258°, while seven rejected permutations have about 85–91° direction errors. The two old assertions that every such fixed case must initialize successfully were therefore too strong. Their updated tests require conservative rejection, bounded one-shot errors, diagnostic counts, and empty public geometry. Large-rotation, forward/lateral, near-planar, and nonuniform-feature controls remain. All 44 tests pass. An initializer can wait for stronger motion in future sequence tracking; M3 may add pair selection without changing M2's ground-truth isolation or arbitrary scale.

## M1 — Initial design choices

1. **Independent project.** The supplied GitHub repository is ROS/catkin material, while this project targets inspectable Python/OpenCV. No source was transplanted. The local task directory was empty and not a Git checkout when inspected.
2. **Narrow image abstraction.** `ImageSample`, `ImageSequence`, `KittiGraySequence`, and `SyntheticSequence` are enough for two-image processing. `Frame`, `Keyframe`, `MapPoint`, and `Map` wait until observation identities, poses, and ownership rules are clear.
3. **Camera convention.** `CalibratedCamera` stores a zero-skew pinhole matrix in pixels. KITTI odometry `P0` supplies the left rectified grayscale camera intrinsic block; `image_0` determines image dimensions. This milestone assumes rectified images and does not handle radial distortion or other camera models.
4. **No pose leakage.** The loader reads images, `P0`, and optional timestamps only. It has no ground-truth pose path or scale input.
5. **ORB and matching.** OpenCV ORB provides keypoints and 256-bit descriptors. Brute-force Hamming KNN provides two candidates; Lowe-style 0.75 ratio filtering removes ambiguous neighbors. A reverse nearest-neighbor consistency check removes duplicate/nonmutual assignments. These are descriptor filters, not geometric verification. Alternatives considered: pure cross-check (simpler but no ambiguity ratio) and optical flow (useful later, but not this ORB descriptor milestone).
6. **Diagnostics as a stable boundary.** Stage counts are written to JSON. Unknown future fields are `null`; tracking is `not_estimated`. This prevents the current match count from being mistaken for essential-matrix/PnP inliers.
7. **Synthetic fallback.** Fixed-seed grayscale texture plus a fixed image translation gives a repeatable demonstration and tests. It is not a 3D scene, an initialization test, or a scale benchmark.
8. **Configuration and logging.** `configs/default.json` contains ORB, match, and drawing settings. The CLI uses Python logging for progress and JSON for persistent measurements. No GUI is required.

## Decisions identified after M1 (pose and initialization items resolved in M2 below)

- Define camera-to-world versus world-to-camera transforms and frame-index/observation conventions before geometry code.
- Decide initialization parallax, cheirality, and reprojection gates using synthetic geometry tests, then a fixed KITTI development slice.
- Fix gauge freedoms explicitly in sliding-window BA. Keep the active window near 5–10 keyframes, use `least_squares` with sparse Jacobian structure, and measure the tradeoff between accuracy and runtime.
- Separate online estimation from offline evaluation modules. An Umeyama Sim(3) alignment may be used for ATE only; scale drift remains a reported limitation.
- Loop closure is optional after the core system and failure analysis work.

## M2 coordinate and scale convention (established before geometry implementation)

- `T_cw` maps world coordinates into camera coordinates: `X_c = R_cw X_w + t_cw`. The first camera defines the arbitrary world frame, so `R1 = I` and `t1 = 0`.
- The recovered second pose is `T_c2w = (R_21, t_21)`, where `X_c2 = R_21 X_c1 + t_21`. Its center in first-camera/world coordinates is `C2 = -R_21.T @ t_21`.
- Pixel-coordinate projection and triangulation use `P1 = K[I|0]`, `P2 = K[R_21|t_21]`. A normalized ray is `normalize(K^-1 [u,v,1])`; the second ray is rotated into the first frame by `R_21.T` before parallax is measured. Intrinsics are applied exactly once.
- `recoverPose` supplies only translation direction. Normalize `t_21` to unit length and use one arbitrary baseline unit for triangulation. No KITTI pose, synthetic depth, or other ground truth sets online scale. Synthetic truth is used only in tests/offline reports.
- Preserve original match-list indices through essential RANSAC, pose recovery, and triangulation. The RANSAC mask remains separate from the cheirality/pose mask and final accepted-point mask.

## M2 calibrated geometry and acceptance decisions

- `findEssentialMat` receives **pixel** correspondences and `K` once, with RANSAC confidence 0.999 and threshold 1.5 **pixels**. Its original mask is preserved. OpenCV may return vertically or horizontally stacked essential candidates; reject malformed/nonfinite matrices and choose the valid candidate with the most pose-recovery inliers. `recoverPose` runs with the RANSAC mask and also receives pixel coordinates plus `K`. Its cheirality mask is recorded separately.
- Initialization requires at least 8 inputs to attempt a five-point RANSAC solution, 40 essential inliers and 40% inlier ratio, 30 pose inliers, and 25 accepted points. These minimum counts avoid initializing from a tiny coincidental consensus. Lower counts reject explicitly; they can be tuned on a fixed development set, not per frame.
- Triangulation uses pixel projection matrices `K[I|0]` and `K[R_21|t_21]`. Filters retain finite homogeneous points with nonzero `w`, positive depth in both cameras, depth at most 200 **unit baselines**, ray parallax at least 0.5° per point and 1.0° median, and reprojection error at most 3.0 **pixels in each image**. The upper depth bound guards unstable far points; the parallax bound rejects small-baseline geometry even when pixel displacement comes from rotation. Camera-2 rays are rotated into camera-1 coordinates before the ray angle is calculated.
- A homography is a conservative degeneracy diagnostic, not a replacement for the essential matrix. It rejects only if its RANSAC consensus covers at least 90% of inputs, exceeds the essential consensus by at least 5%, **and** candidate median parallax is below 2°. This catches a noiseless pure-rotation case that can otherwise yield a spurious translation; it may also reject genuinely planar, low-parallax initialization, which is preferable to an unstable sparse map. Planar scenes with sufficient parallax are not rejected merely for homography support.
- The synthetic benchmark contains 450 visible noncoplanar landmarks at depths 4–12 scene units, baseline 0.8 scene units, nominal rotation 3°, Gaussian pixel noise 0.35, seed 7. Truth generates observations and evaluates results afterward; the estimator receives only 2D coordinates and calibration. Seed 7 produced 1.246° rotation error, 1.842° translation-direction error, 427 essential/pose inliers, 427 accepted points, 4.039° median parallax, and 0.261/0.261 px median reprojection errors. Pose bias can create sizable raw 3D depth error; an offline single-scale alignment gives 0.565 scene-unit median 3D error. That alignment is evaluation only and never changes the estimated PLY/map.
- An image-based test renders independent textured landmarks at valid projected positions. M1 ORB plus descriptor filtering retained 348 matches; geometry accepted 260 points with 0.165°/0.686° rotation/direction error. Rendered sprites are a controlled software test, not natural-image realism. The original M1 translated-image fallback remains only a matching test. Pure rotation, low baseline, and heavy correspondence outliers reject under the current gates. The later genuine KITTI measurements are recorded below.
- `landmarks.ply` contains 3D points in camera-1 world coordinates, with unknown scale; `poses.json` contains both `T_cw` matrices and world-frame camera centers. The PNG map is an oblique 2D projection of those 3D coordinates. These exports are interoperable with a future optional Blender script; no Blender or GPU package enters the estimator. Optional CUDA work, if ever justified, should use an isolated environment and verify actual GPU access first; `nvidia-smi`'s CUDA compatibility is not a Toolkit version.

## M2 — Offline KITTI truth convention and pose-stability audit
